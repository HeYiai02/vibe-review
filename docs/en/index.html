Turn spectrum

1 session · each bar is one turn

Bar height is how many tools that turn used; colour is the quality score: greener is smoother, redder is a crash. The shape is front-loaded — the first two days ate 100% of the work (114 of 114 calls); everything after was polishing.

Smooth (q≥70) Mixed (40–69) Crash (<40) Cyan cap = this turn wrote files
q is computed, not judged by a model: base 70, +10 for a successful file write, −12 per tool error, −10 if the turn was interrupted, +8 if you carried on the next turn, −12 if you changed direction. It measures whether the turn moved the project forward and whether it left debt behind — not how you felt about it.

Why it kept coming back

You said "it's ugly, make it like the Apple Store". I read that as two cards, image left and form right; what you actually wanted was one long scrolling image with a sticky add-to-cart bar. The form was never agreed, so every version after that just polished details in the wrong direction.

Using AI next time

For anything the user can see, name the form before writing code: cards, a long page, or layered? Once that is confirmed, I start.

We're redoing the order page. Don't write code yet — answer me four things:
1. Does the product image fit one screen, or is it a long page you scroll?
2. Is add-to-cart a button in the form, or a bar stuck to the bottom?
3. Under 420px, how do image and form stack?
4. By "like the Apple Store", do you mean lots of whitespace, or big images?
Confirm those four and then start.
